# Question catalog — quick reference

Example questions to offer when the user asks what they can ask this skill, or what they
can do with the Simple Booking connectors — pick a handful from the relevant category,
don't dump the whole list. Answer in the user's language, as always — this file being
English does not change that.

Generated from `dev/question-catalog.json` by `dev/build_question_catalog.py`: do not edit by hand.

## PA — Pace and performance

- **PA-1.** How is September going? Are we ahead or behind last year?
- **PA-2.** How does the on-the-books for the next six months compare with the same period last year, month by month?
- **PA-3.** How many bookings came in this week, and what are they worth?
- **PA-4.** Show me the pick-up day by day over the last 30 days.
- **PA-5.** How much do I already hold for the last quarter of the year?
- **PA-6.** How does this month's pick-up compare with the same month last year?
- **PA-7.** What is the monthly trend of room revenue over the last 12 months?

## DI — Direct vs Intermediated

- **DI-1.** What is direct worth compared with the OTAs in September?
- **DI-2.** Is direct really more profitable, or does it just sell better rooms?
- **DI-3.** How has the direct/intermediated mix changed over the last twelve months?

## PI — Portals and indirect channels

- **PI-1.** What do the portals actually cost me?
- **PI-2.** Which portals bring me the most volume, and at what ADR each?
- **PI-3.** What is the real OTA mix, with readable names instead of channel codes?
- **PI-4.** How much revenue comes through Booking, and how much through Expedia?

## TD — Tracking your direct channel

- **TD-1.** How is my direct channel split between website, mobile, manual entry and tracked links?
- **TD-2.** How much weight do bookings keyed in by staff carry?
- **TD-3.** How much of my direct comes from metasearch (Google Hotel Ads, Trivago) tracked through a link?
- **TD-4.** How many direct bookings has the website's AI assistant brought me?

## RR — Rooms, rates and packages

- **RR-1.** Which room type earns me the most, and what share of revenue does each one carry?
- **RR-2.** How wide is the price range on each room type?
- **RR-3.** Which rate plans is my revenue concentrated on?
- **RR-4.** Which offers are working, and which are not?
- **RR-5.** Which packages do I actually sell?
- **RR-6.** How much do I sell at rack rate, how much on offer and how much as a package?
- **RR-7.** What is my average ADR in September, and how does it compare with last year?

## BB — Booking behaviour

- **BB-1.** How far ahead do I sell?
- **BB-2.** At what time of day do bookings come in?
- **BB-3.** Which days of the week do I receive the most bookings on?
- **BB-4.** How much of my business arrives last minute?
- **BB-5.** What length of stay am I selling? Does a weekend minimum stay make sense?
- **BB-6.** Which nights of the month have the most single-night stays, and would it be worth closing them to new arrivals?
- **BB-7.** Is lead time different between direct and OTA?
- **BB-8.** How many nights do I sell per booking on average, and how many rooms?
- **BB-9.** How are bookings distributed between short and long stays?

## CA — Cancellations

- **CA-1.** Who cancels on me, and how late?
- **CA-2.** What is my cancellation rate, channel by channel?
- **CA-3.** How much revenue have I lost to cancellations this year, compared with last year?
- **CA-4.** How long passes between booking and cancellation?
- **CA-5.** How many cancellations arrive less than seven days before arrival?
- **CA-6.** Do non-refundable rates cancel less?

## GU — Your guests

- **GU-1.** How many bookings include children, and what are they worth compared with the rest?
- **GU-2.** Which source markets do my guests come from?
- **GU-3.** Do I sell more to couples or to families?
- **GU-4.** How many multi-room bookings do I have, and what share of revenue do they carry?
- **GU-5.** How many guests per booking on average?
- **GU-6.** Do foreign markets buy different room types than Italians?
- **GU-7.** How has the market mix changed compared with last year?

## AS — Ancillary services

- **AS-1.** How much do I sell beyond the room?
- **AS-2.** Which ancillary services sell the most, and which earn the most?
- **AS-3.** Is the most-booked service also the most profitable one, or are they two different rankings?
- **AS-4.** Has ancillary penetration grown compared with last year?

## PC — Payments and collections

- **PC-1.** How well secured are my bookings? How much has actually been collected?
- **PC-2.** Which payment methods do my direct guests use?
- **PC-3.** How many bookings arrive with no payment method recorded at all?
- **PC-4.** How many bookings require manual bank-transfer reconciliation, and what are they worth?
- **PC-5.** Which payment gateway processes my bookings, and how much does each collect?

## TT — Today and tomorrow

- **TT-1.** How many arrivals do I have tomorrow?
- **TT-2.** How many people do I have in house tonight?
- **TT-3.** Which are the busiest days next month?
- **TT-4.** How many departures do I have this weekend?

## MP — Multiple properties

- **MP-1.** Compare me with another property in the group: direct share, ADR and length of stay.
- **MP-2.** Which property in the group has the highest direct share?
- **MP-3.** Which properties in the portfolio are selling better or worse than the same period last year?
- **MP-4.** Which property has the highest cost of intermediation (OTA commission)?

## CV — Bookings from Converto quotes

- **CV-1.** How much are bookings from a Converto quote worth, and what share of direct revenue do they represent?
- **CV-2.** What share closes on its own with the guest on the mini-site, and what share is instead brought to a close by staff on the guest's instruction? How much is each worth?
- **CV-3.** Is the average length of stay for Converto bookings different from the rest of direct?
- **CV-4.** Do Converto bookings come from different source markets than the rest of direct?
- **CV-5.** Do Converto bookings show a different guest profile (families with children, couples...) compared with the rest of direct?
- **CV-6.** Is the cancellation rate for Converto bookings different from the rest of direct?
